TASK 2 — PHISHING AWARENESS TRAINING
Project: SecureWise Ultra PRO

Purpose
A multi-section cybersecurity awareness website covering phishing, social engineering, identity security, defensive investigation, reporting and scenario-based learning.

Includes
- Dashboard, phishing email inspection, fake websites/URLs, social engineering and everyday protection.
- Video/resource library with embedded CISA phishing-awareness videos and direct-open links, plus official FTC/CISA resources.
- Safe fictional phishing simulation and incident-response playbook.
- QR phishing, SMS/phone scams, workplace fraud, passwords/MFA, privacy/device safety, scenario practice and glossary/FAQ.
- AI/deepfake awareness, cloud/collaboration security, mobile security.
- SOC analyst decision lab, threat-intelligence concepts and local incident-report draft builder.
- Email forensics, identity attack defence and interactive security decision drills.
- 35-question scenario-based final assessment with feedback, explanations and retry.
- Browser-local progress, report export and printable completion certificate.

Technology
HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No backend/database required.

Video notes
- Video embeds require internet access and may be blocked by browser, network or publisher settings.
- Each featured training video has a direct YouTube link as a fallback.
- The website cannot guarantee playback if YouTube or a network blocks the content.

Safety and limitations
- Educational awareness only; not a live phishing detector, SIEM, email filter or production incident-response platform.
- The simulation uses fictional data; no credentials are collected and no emails are sent.
- The incident report builder creates local draft text only; it does not submit reports.
- Progress is stored locally in the browser and is not synced to a server.
- Never include passwords, MFA codes or unnecessary personal information in report drafts.
- Follow official organizational security procedures for real incidents.

Run
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Keep index.html, styles.css and script.js together.
3. Open index.html in a modern browser.
